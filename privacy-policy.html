Battery Guardian Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Battery Guardian respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how your information is handled when you use Battery Guardian.

1. About Battery Guardian

Battery Guardian is a battery protection application designed to monitor the device battery level and help users avoid excessive battery drain by displaying a charging protection screen when the configured battery threshold is reached.

2. Information the App Accesses

Battery Guardian may access the following device information to provide its core functionality:

  • Current battery percentage.
  • Whether the device is currently charging.
  • Power connection and disconnection events.
  • Whether Battery Guardian's protection feature is enabled.
  • The emergency gesture configured by the user.
  • Temporary unlock status and its expiration time.

This information is used only to operate the battery protection features of the application.

3. Accessibility Service

Battery Guardian provides an optional Android Accessibility Service integration.

When the user manually enables the Accessibility Service, Battery Guardian may receive accessibility window-change events. This allows the application to detect changes in the currently active application or window and help restore the Battery Guardian charging protection screen when the configured battery threshold has been reached.

Battery Guardian does not use the Accessibility Service to read messages, passwords, typed text, or the contents of other applications. It does not perform gestures or automatically control other applications.

Accessibility access is optional and is used only to support the battery protection functionality described above.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Battery Guardian stores certain settings locally on your device, including:

  • Battery protection threshold.
  • Emergency gesture.
  • Protection enabled or disabled state.
  • Temporary emergency unlock state.

These settings are stored locally using Android application storage and are not transmitted to our servers by the app.

5. Data Collection

Battery Guardian does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, messages, passwords, or precise location.

Battery Guardian does not require an account or user registration for its core functionality.

6. Data Sharing

Battery Guardian does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The application does not intentionally transmit the battery information or locally stored protection settings to an external server.

7. Internet Access

Battery Guardian's core battery protection functionality does not require an internet connection.

If a future version introduces an online service, analytics, advertising, or another third-party service, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before or when that functionality becomes applicable.

8. Battery Optimization

Battery Guardian may provide an option that opens Android's battery optimization settings so that the user can decide whether to allow the application to operate with fewer battery optimization restrictions.

The decision to change battery optimization settings remains under the user's control through Android system settings.

9. Notifications

Battery Guardian may use notifications while its battery monitoring service is active. Notifications are used to make the ongoing battery monitoring service visible to the user.

10. Data Security

Battery Guardian is designed to keep its settings and protection state locally on the user's device.

Users should also keep their Android device updated and protected with the device's built-in security features.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Battery Guardian does not maintain a remote user account or server-side personal data for its core functionality.

Locally stored application settings can be removed by disabling the protection feature, resetting the application settings, clearing the application's data, or uninstalling Battery Guardian.

12. Children's Privacy

Battery Guardian is not specifically directed toward children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, or applicable privacy requirements change.

The updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.

14. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Battery Guardian, please contact the developer.
